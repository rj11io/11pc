# 11blog logo

Geometric double-one mark plus a compact, mono-inspired `blog` wordmark. The
lockup reads as 11blog without repeating the number. A small green square acts
as a signal or cursor: a restrained reference to the product-engineering and
technology focus, not a literal technology icon.

Construction follows the wider brand direction:

- sharp geometry and square corners;
- foreground white on the default dark background;
- green used as a small, deliberate signal;
- custom geometric letterforms informed by the interface's monospaced typography;
- a simple silhouette that remains legible at small sizes.

## Assets

- [`11blog-logo.svg`](./11blog-logo.svg): primary horizontal lockup,
  transparent background. For `#0A0A0A` or another sufficiently dark surface.
- [`11blog-logo-dark.svg`](./11blog-logo-dark.svg): primary lockup with padding
  on the default `#0A0A0A` background.
- [`11blog-mark.svg`](./11blog-mark.svg): standalone square mark on the default
  dark background. Avatars and compact placements.

## Usage

- Clear space around the logo: at least the width of the green signal square.
- Horizontal lockup: no less than 160 pixels wide.
- Standalone mark: no less than 24 × 24 pixels.
- Do not round the container, stretch the artwork, add shadows, or change
  individual colors.
- The wordmark is already vector shapes. No font file required.
